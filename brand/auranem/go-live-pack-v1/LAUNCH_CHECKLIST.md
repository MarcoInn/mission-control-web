# AURANEM Go-Live Pre-Flight Checklist

## 1) Creative QA
- [ ] Final Hero is 1080x1350 and exports clean (no artifacting)
- [ ] Final Ad is 1080x1350 and text is readable on mobile
- [ ] Final UGC is 9:16 and duration is 15–20s (target: ~18s)
- [ ] Product render shows **white lid** and realistic **60 CAPS** proportion
- [ ] Same typography, color palette, and CTA button style across all assets
- [ ] Premium-natural look maintained (no clinical/medical visual cues)

## 2) Compliance & Claims
- [ ] No medical/disease claims in visual or caption copy
- [ ] "Food supplement • Read label before use" present where needed
- [ ] Landing page copy aligned with supplement-safe messaging
- [ ] Legal disclaimer checked for DE/EN market requirements

## 3) Platform Readiness
- [ ] Meta Feed: 4:5 static upload confirmed
- [ ] Reels/Stories/TikTok: 9:16 video upload confirmed
- [ ] Thumbnail/cover frame selected from CTA-friendly moment
- [ ] Caption language variant selected (DE or EN)
- [ ] UTM parameters attached to destination URL

## 4) Audio & Technical
- [ ] Background music source is rights-safe
- [ ] Audio level is low enough for VO/caption focus
- [ ] Video export H.264 + AAC tested on iOS and Android
- [ ] First 2 seconds are visually strong (hook test)

## 5) Launch Ops
- [ ] Campaign names standardized (Hero / Ad / UGC)
- [ ] Tracking events verified (view content / add to cart / purchase)
- [ ] Initial budget split prepared
- [ ] Reporting dashboard columns set (CTR, hold rate, CPC, CPA)

## 6) Post-Launch 24h Check
- [ ] UGC hold rate @3s reviewed
- [ ] Thumb-stop rate (first frame impact) reviewed
- [ ] CTR by creative compared
- [ ] First winner/loser decisions logged
