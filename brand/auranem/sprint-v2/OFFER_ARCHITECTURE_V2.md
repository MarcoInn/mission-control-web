# AURANEM — OFFER ARCHITECTURE V2

## Executive Intent
Increase conversion confidence by making the buying path obvious:
- **Starter = low-friction first commitment**
- **Plus = better value for planned consistency**
- **Subscription = highest convenience for repeat buyers (opt-in, cancel-anytime)**

All offer language stays compliance-safe (no medical promises).

---

## 1) Offer Stack

## A) Starter (Entry)
- **Name:** Daily Balance Starter
- **Duration:** 30 days
- **Price Frame:** from **€89** (reference €119)
- **Use Case:** First structured trial with minimal decision pressure
- **Includes:**
  - 1× 30-day unit
  - Start guide (daily anchor setup)
  - Weekly checkpoint sheet (Week 1–4)
- **Primary Promise (safe):** Build a clearer daily routine with less friction

## B) Plus (Core Value)
- **Name:** Daily Balance Plus
- **Duration:** 60 days
- **Price Frame:** from **€149** (reference €189)
- **Unit Economics:** ~€2.48/day vs Starter ~€2.97/day
- **Use Case:** Customers ready for 8-week consistency
- **Includes:**
  - 2× 30-day units (60-day total)
  - Routine card for office/travel
  - Priority support for setup questions (business days)
- **Primary Promise (safe):** Better continuity with fewer re-order interruptions

## C) Subscription (Convenience Tier)
- **Name:** Daily Balance Subscription
- **Cycle:** Every 30 days (default) or every 60 days (optional)
- **Price Frame:**
  - 30-day subscription: **€79** per cycle
  - 60-day subscription: **€139** per cycle
- **Incentive Logic:** Best per-day rate + convenience + reserved stock
- **Includes:**
  - Auto-delivery schedule
  - Pause/skip/cancel in account settings
  - Loyalty benefit after 3 successful cycles (e.g., free shipping window or bonus routine asset)
- **Primary Promise (safe):** Keep routine momentum with less admin overhead

---

## 2) Price Framing Logic

## Ladder Strategy
1. **Anchor:** Show reference pricing (UVP) first.
2. **Decision Price:** Show current offer price clearly.
3. **Per-Day Clarity:** Translate each tier to daily cost.
4. **Best-Value Highlight:** Plus or Subscription visibly marked as “Best Value” depending on intent.

## Recommended UI Copy
- **Starter:** “Best for first 30 days”
- **Plus:** “Best value for planned consistency”
- **Subscription:** “Best convenience for repeat routine”

## Psychology Rules
- Avoid discount hype language; use calm premium framing.
- Show **total price + per-day price** side-by-side.
- Keep upgrade path explicit: Starter → Plus → Subscription.

## 3) Risk-Reversal Copy (Compliance-Safe)

## Core Risk-Reversal Block
- “No automatic subscription on Starter/Plus.”
- “You review full price and order details before final confirmation.”
- “Transparent ingredients and batch labeling.”
- “Cancel/pause anytime for Subscription in your account.”

## Optional Checkout Reassurance Line
“Your decision stays in your control at every step—no hidden commitments.”

## 4) FAQ Objections (Conversion-Critical)

### Q1: Is this a subscription?
**A:** Starter and Plus are one-time purchases. Subscription is optional and clearly labeled as opt-in.

### Q2: Why is Plus better value?
**A:** Plus lowers daily cost and reduces reorder friction for people already planning 8 weeks.

### Q3: What if I’m unsure where to begin?
**A:** Start with Starter for a structured 30-day entry. You can move to Plus later.

### Q4: Can I cancel subscription anytime?
**A:** Yes. You can pause, skip, or cancel from your account settings before the next billing cycle.

### Q5: Do you make medical promises?
**A:** No. AURANEM does not claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

### Q6: How do I know what I’ll pay?
**A:** Product page and checkout display total price before final confirmation.

## 5) Launch Recommendation
1. Keep Starter/Plus live as default decision pair.
2. Introduce Subscription as a post-purchase upgrade first (lower trust friction).
3. After data validates adoption, move Subscription into primary comparison grid.
