# NEM Static Concept v3 (1080x1350) — What changed

## Objective
Refined the bonus static into a cleaner premium-natural layout with clearer hierarchy, improved spacing, and a stronger CTA treatment while keeping compliance-safe wording.

## Key improvements

1. **Typography hierarchy improved**
   - Headline kept as the dominant element (large serif, 3-line structure).
   - Supporting copy moved into a distinct secondary tier (smaller sans-serif, two lines).
   - CTA text separated in a high-contrast button block.

2. **Alignment, margins, whitespace**
   - Introduced a consistent left alignment system for headline, body copy, CTA, and brand line.
   - Added larger breathing room between headline and body text.
   - Reduced visual crowding around the product area to improve scan flow.

3. **Cleaner CTA treatment**
   - Replaced the previous cluttered CTA handling with a single clean dark button.
   - Increased legibility via stronger contrast and centered label.
   - Kept wording compliance-safe: **"Starter unverbindlich prüfen"**.

4. **Premium-natural visual tone retained**
   - Kept soft neutral background and warm panel tones.
   - Preserved product visibility with a clear right-side silhouette and restrained accent label.
   - Avoided extra decorative clutter to maintain a high-end, minimal look.
