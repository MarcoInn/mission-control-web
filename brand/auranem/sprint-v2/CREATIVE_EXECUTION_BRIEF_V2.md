## 5) Production Checklist (Practical)

## Creative
- [ ] First frame communicates angle in <2 seconds
- [ ] Text safe area for 9:16 and 4:5 crops
- [ ] Premium-natural look (neutral tones, soft contrast, minimal clutter)

## Copy & Compliance
- [ ] No medical or disease claims
- [ ] No guarantee language (“heilt”, “wirkt sicher”, etc.)
- [ ] Includes transparent offer framing

## Conversion
- [ ] CTA appears by second 8–10 in short videos
- [ ] Starter and Plus intent path clearly separated
- [ ] Landing headline aligned with ad hook

## Measurement
- [ ] UTM by angle/hook/script ID
- [ ] Naming convention: `v2_[angle]_[hook]_[date]`
- [ ] Daily reporting template ready (CTR, CVR, CAC)
