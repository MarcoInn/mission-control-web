# Content Lab - Current Cycle: Static Ad (Performance Focus)

## 1. Selected Format: Ad Static (Instagram/Facebook Feed)
**Goal:** Create a scroll-stopping static ad for AURANEM "Focus" supplement.
**Target Audience:** Students/Professionals needing deep work capability.
**Value Prop:** "Clean energy, no jitters."

## 2. Solution Approaches

### Approach A: The "Minimalist Science" Style
*   **Visual:** Clean white background, single bottle of AURANEM Focus centered, soft shadow. Overlay text in bold, sans-serif font (Helvetica/Inter style).
*   **Copy:** "Brain fog isn't mandatory."
*   **Color Palette:** Clinical white, deep blue (trust), subtle silver accents.
*   **Vibe:** Professional, pharmaceutical-grade, trustworthy.

### Approach B: The "Lifestyle Outcome" Style
*   **Visual:** Close-up of a cluttered desk (laptop, notebook, coffee) but everything is perfectly organized. A hand holding the AURANEM bottle is entering the frame from the side. Warm, golden-hour lighting.
*   **Copy:** "Your 3 PM deadline just got easier."
*   **Color Palette:** Warm wood tones, soft amber light, brand green for nature/health connection.
*   **Vibe:** Relatable, aspirational, productivity-focused.

## 3. Scorecard Comparison

| Metric | Approach A (Minimalist) | Approach B (Lifestyle) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook (Scroll-Stop)** | 7/10 - Very clean, stands out against busy feeds. | 8/10 - Warmth draws eye, but desk setups are common. |
| **Readability** | 9/10 - Text on white is unbeatable. | 7/10 - Text needs background box or shadow to pop. |
| **Authenticity** | 8/10 - Feels like a premium product. | 6/10 - Can feel like stock photography if not careful. |
| **Brand Fit** | 9/10 - Matches AURANEM's "clean science" ethos. | 7/10 - A bit too generic "study vibes". |
| **CTA Clarity** | 9/10 - Button/URL clearly visible. | 8/10 - Good, but competes with image details. |
| **TOTAL SCORE** | **42/50** | **36/50** |

## 4. Decision
**Winner:** Approach A (Minimalist Science).
**Reason:** Higher readability and stronger alignment with the premium/clean brand identity. The "Lifestyle" approach risks looking like generic stock content without a custom shoot.

## 5. Next Steps
*   Generate high-res mockup of Approach A.
*   Test 2 headline variants: "Brain fog isn't mandatory" vs. "Focus on demand."

## Cycle 2026-03-03 22:33 UTC — Selected Format: UGC 15–20s (MOF)

**Goal:** Create a compliance-safe, non-slideshow UGC concept that feels native and drives click intent for AURANEM.

### 1) Two solution approaches (free/open-first)

### Approach A — "Routine Rescue" (single-take, handheld authenticity)
- **Production stack (free/open):** Smartphone camera + CapCut free (cuts, captions) + natural room light.
- **Structure (18s):**
  - 0–3s Hook: "Ich war jeden Nachmittag im Fokus-Loch."
  - 3–10s Routine context: desk scene, water glass, bottle opening, one natural motion sequence.
  - 10–15s Outcome framing: "Jetzt: gleiche To-do-Liste, aber klarerer Ablauf in meiner Routine."
  - 15–18s CTA: "Wenn du Struktur statt Chaos willst: AURANEM in deine Morgenroutine testen."
- **Visual style:** Real desk, slight camera shake, one continuous movement to avoid slideshow feel.
- **Compliance-safe copy angle:** routine consistency + general wellbeing support, no medical/disease claims.

### Approach B — "2-State Contrast" (before/after micro-story, dynamic cut rhythm)
- **Production stack (free/open):** Smartphone + DaVinci Resolve free (speed ramp + text overlays).
- **Structure (19s):**
  - 0–2s Hook split-screen text: "Chaos-Modus vs. Klarheits-Modus"
  - 2–8s "Before": cluttered desk, repeated task switching, muted color grade.
  - 8–15s "After": same person, simplified setup + AURANEM next to water, warmer grade.
  - 15–19s CTA: "Starte mit einer konsistenten Routine. Mehr bei AURANEM."
- **Visual style:** Fast cuts + sound design accents, still creator-native (not polished ad studio).
- **Compliance-safe copy angle:** clarity in everyday routine, no guaranteed result language.

### 2) Scorecard comparison

| Metric | Approach A (Routine Rescue) | Approach B (2-State Contrast) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook** | 8/10 — strong first-person pain line | 9/10 — immediate visual contrast grabs attention |
| **Readability** | 9/10 — simple captions, clear pacing | 7/10 — faster edits can reduce text retention |
| **Authenticity** | 9/10 — native UGC feel, believable setting | 7/10 — effect-heavy edits risk ad-like feel |
| **Brand Fit** | 8/10 — routine + clarity anchor fits | 9/10 — clearly maps "Routine statt Chaos" |
| **CTA** | 8/10 — direct and natural | 8/10 — clear, but slightly generic |
| **TOTAL** | **42/50** | **40/50** |

### 3) Decision
- **Winner kept:** Approach A — "Routine Rescue" (**42/50**)
- **Loser archived:** Approach B — "2-State Contrast" (**40/50**)
- **Why loser lost:** Better top-of-feed hook, but readability and authenticity drop due to edit intensity; higher risk of "produced ad" perception vs trusted UGC.

- Free/open-first tools used in concepting ✅
- Supplement-safe language (no cure/disease/guarantee) ✅
- Anti-slideshow execution (single-take or motion-led cuts) ✅
- No external publishing performed ✅
