# Content Lab - Current Cycle: Static Ad (Performance Focus)

## 1. Selected Format: Ad Static (Instagram/Facebook Feed)
**Goal:** Create a scroll-stopping static ad for AURANEM "Focus" supplement.
**Target Audience:** Students/Professionals needing deep work capability.
**Value Prop:** "Clean energy, no jitters."

## 2. Solution Approaches

### Approach A: The "Minimalist Science" Style
*   **Visual:** Clean white background, single bottle of AURANEM Focus centered, soft shadow. Overlay text in bold, sans-serif font (Helvetica/Inter style).
*   **Copy:** "Brain fog isn't mandatory."
*   **Color Palette:** Clinical white, deep blue (trust), subtle silver accents.
*   **Vibe:** Professional, pharmaceutical-grade, trustworthy.

### Approach B: The "Lifestyle Outcome" Style
*   **Visual:** Close-up of a cluttered desk (laptop, notebook, coffee) but everything is perfectly organized. A hand holding the AURANEM bottle is entering the frame from the side. Warm, golden-hour lighting.
*   **Copy:** "Your 3 PM deadline just got easier."
*   **Color Palette:** Warm wood tones, soft amber light, brand green for nature/health connection.
*   **Vibe:** Relatable, aspirational, productivity-focused.

## 3. Scorecard Comparison

| Metric | Approach A (Minimalist) | Approach B (Lifestyle) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook (Scroll-Stop)** | 7/10 - Very clean, stands out against busy feeds. | 8/10 - Warmth draws eye, but desk setups are common. |
| **Readability** | 9/10 - Text on white is unbeatable. | 7/10 - Text needs background box or shadow to pop. |
| **Authenticity** | 8/10 - Feels like a premium product. | 6/10 - Can feel like stock photography if not careful. |
| **Brand Fit** | 9/10 - Matches AURANEM's "clean science" ethos. | 7/10 - A bit too generic "study vibes". |
| **CTA Clarity** | 9/10 - Button/URL clearly visible. | 8/10 - Good, but competes with image details. |
| **TOTAL SCORE** | **42/50** | **36/50** |

## 4. Decision
**Winner:** Approach A (Minimalist Science).
**Reason:** Higher readability and stronger alignment with the premium/clean brand identity. The "Lifestyle" approach risks looking like generic stock content without a custom shoot.
