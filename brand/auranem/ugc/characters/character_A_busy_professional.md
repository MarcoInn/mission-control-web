# Character A — Busy Professional

## Persona Card
- **Archetype:** Busy Professional (28–40, performance-focused, schedule-packed)
- **Tone:** Direct, efficient, credible, no fluff
- **Core pain points:** Midday energy dips, inconsistent supplement routine, decision fatigue, no time to research stacks
- **Hook style:** Time-saving promise + “finally structured” angle
- **On-camera style:** Fast cuts, desk/kitchen transitions, calm confidence, product-in-hand closeups

## Script Variant 1 (15s) — “No Time, Still Consistent”
**Hook (0–2s):** “Wenn dein Kalender voll ist, brauchst du kein komplexes Stack.”
**Problem (2–6s):** “Ich hab Supplements ständig gewechselt und am Ende oft vergessen.”
**Solution (6–12s):** “Mit AURANEM hab ich eine klare Routine in unter 10 Sekunden am Morgen.”
**CTA (12–15s):** “Wenn du busy bist: Starter oder Plus auswählen und heute starten.”

### Shotlist
1. Calendar/phone with meetings (0–2s)
2. Desk scene, creator shows 2–3 random bottles (2–5s)
3. Creator grabs AURANEM bottle at kitchen counter (5–9s)
4. One-step routine demo + sip water (9–12s)
5. Confident selfie + bottle to camera + CTA subtitle (12–15s)
